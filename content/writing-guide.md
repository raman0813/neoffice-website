# NeOffice writing guide (for Claude and anyone writing posts)

Edit this file to change how new blog posts are written.

## Who we write for
HR, admin and facilities heads, and IT managers at mid-size and large companies running hybrid offices,
mostly in India, plus global buyers. They are busy and practical. They want to know what to do and what it costs.

## Voice
- Plain, friendly, confident. Short sentences. No hype words ("revolutionize", "game-changer", "unlock").
- Use British/Indian spelling (utilisation, organisation).
- Talk about real problems: return-to-office rules, desk shortages, cab safety, visitor queues, measuring office use.
- Don't invent customer names, quotes or statistics. If a number is needed, use one from our website or say "in our experience".

## Shape of a post
- 900 to 1,300 words.
- Title under 65 characters that says what the reader gets.
- A one-sentence summary (the `description` field) under 155 characters for Google.
- Open with the reader's problem in 2 to 3 sentences. Then 4 to 7 sections with `##` headings.
- Use bullet lists where they help. Finish with a short "How NeOffice helps" section and a link to /schedule-demo/.
- Link to 1 or 2 relevant NeOffice pages, for example /workspace/desk-booking-software/,
  /employee-transport-management-system/, /workspace/visitor-management-system/, /pricing-plans/.
