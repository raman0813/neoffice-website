## Introduction

[Visitor management systems](/workspace/visitor-management-system/) have been around for a long time, but they are still far from ubiquitous. The average security guard can't keep track of who's coming in and out of your office, which is why you're probably missing out on a lot of opportunities to impress your guests.

If you want to streamline your security and impress your guests, it's time you invest in an automated visitor management system. These visitors might include delivery people, contractors, guests of employees and more. Visitor management systems include features that can help you streamline security and enhance the visitor experience to impress your guests.

### Streamlining security

Visitor management systems are designed to help streamline security, enhance the visitor experience and impress your guests. With a visitor management system, you can:

-   [Streamline security](/revolutionize-office-experience-with-cutting-edge-visitor-management-software/) by reducing wait times in line while improving customer service. Visitors don't have to wait in long lines before they can get into your facility or building because they don't have to check in at all! The system will also make sure everyone is checked against watch lists so no one gets through who shouldn't be there.
-   Enhance the visitor experience by saving time and frustration on both sides of entry points like doors or gates (think airport checkpoints). Visitors won't have to worry about forgetting something important at home because they'll already have it stored digitally with their profile; meanwhile employees won't spend hours manually checking people in anymore either!

### Access Control

Access control is the ability to control who can enter a building, area or facility. It's used to manage security, safety and convenience of people using a building. Access control allows for the management of who can enter a building or area. It also enables managers to restrict access by certain individuals at certain times of the day or week.

Access Control systems are made up of both hardware and software components that work together in order to provide you with an effective solution for your needs.

### Entry/Exit Logging

Entry/exit logging is a feature of visitor management systems that allows you to track who enters and exits the building, as well as keep track of when people enter and leave. This feature is useful for keeping tabs on who is in your facility at any given time, especially if you have multiple entrances or exits (or both).

### Photo Capture and Scanning

A photo capture and scanning system is a great tool for any security officer. The ability to take photos of guests and quickly check their identities is invaluable and can be accomplished with just one device. You can also use this technology to track the movement of people in and out of your facility, which makes it easier for you or other staff members who might be monitoring the system from afar.

### Improve your services

Visitor management systems can help you improve visitor services and increase security at the same time as saving you time, money and energy.

Visitor management systems allow you to track who's coming into your building, when they arrive and where they go. This allows you to provide better customer service by anticipating their needs before they even ask for it! For example: if a guest arrives late in the evening or on a weekend or holiday when most other buildings are closed for business but yours is open (with 24/7 access), but with visitor management software installed at your front desk--you'll know exactly what days/times are busiest so that when someone asks if there's anything else we offer besides tours--you'll have all sorts of ideas ready on hand!

By knowing which areas get more foot traffic than others--you can also make sure these areas are well lit & secure too; preventing accidents from happening due to darkness while also deterring criminal activity such as theft because if someone knows there are cameras everywhere watching their every move--they're less likely commit crimes knowing that any wrongdoings will eventually come back around...and perhaps cause legal trouble down the road.
