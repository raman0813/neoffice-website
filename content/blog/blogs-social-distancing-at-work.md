Pandemic has completely changed the way we interact with people. As offices across the globe are planning re-entry to office, the onus is on the administration to ease their staff into the ‘new normal’ of office life while remaining mindful of the important health concerns involved. What will social distancing look like in our workplaces, and how can we ensure that we keep doing our best for ourselves and our co-workers in the long run? are some of the questions to keep in mind while creating a robust social distancing rules and protocols in office.

 Along with planning a staggered approach to enter office, there are many key areas that needs to be focused on to safely return back to work:

1.  Determine the number of employees versus the amount of desk space available. Create an appropriate desk seating chart for physical separation.
2.   Communicate physical distancing guidelines to employees through signages in common areas and entry/exit points.
3.   Implement a contact-tracing mechanism to identify any social interaction and also to track back in case of any medical emergencies.

Hence, there is an impending need for workplaces to adopt a flexible approach towards working, by opting for an integrated workspace management tool is ideal to manage these issues.

 **Communicating workplace best practices:**

Consistent communication with employees will be a key factor in outlining best practices and assistance as they start to return to the office. Signatures and sign-boards carefully placed at important junctures in the workplace can aid in safety reminders on social distancing, good hand hygiene, symptom checks, and desk space cleaning recommendations. Also offer employees other channels of communication, which employees can use to reach out with any concerns they may have.
